# SOURCE8 comparison gauge and literal cache reduction

The frozen source folds have a particularly small M1 dependency. For sources77,101,117,125, respectively kappa=(3/55,0,15/44,3/55), the A response is kappa*alpha23 and the B response is -kappa*beta23. Here alpha23=lambda0*M1_r*e23 and beta23=lambda0*M1_ell*e23-lambda1*M1_r*e23. The source gate checks every full56 first-vector coefficient, not just support names.

The exact adjoints give lambda0*G2_r=0 and lambda0*G2_ell=lambda1*G2_r. These show both scalars are invariant under the actual admissible comparison change M1→M1+G2linear*A0. No canonical mu value or normalizer coefficient is set to zero. In addition, the full56 vector W2 has zero r² and r*ell coefficients (kappa*e23 cancels its negative), while ell² is zero in the base. The remaining z*linear terms reduce to zero for every target. The independent normalizer covariance theorem therefore removes both the unknown target-frame correction and the known bottom quadratic normal form in this family.
