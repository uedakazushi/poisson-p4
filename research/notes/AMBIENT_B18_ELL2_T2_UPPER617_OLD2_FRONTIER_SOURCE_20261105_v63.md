The independently verified formal OLD2 fold has 42 surviving function labels.
Twenty-eight are original physical164 functions. The two plane conductors gA
and gB have order at least three by the accepted literal zero7 source proof,
so their seconds vanish. The other twelve functions occur only in the outer
four OLD point pivots; none of those pivots occurs in any of the 619 constant
sources. Their formal second support is exactly the two ordinary plane pairs

This metadata-only packet records the original C0 arithmetic and the actual
regular `r`/`g` quotient constructors for those twelve functions. Arithmetic
aliases are expanded; quotient constructors remain opaque. In particular the
four restored coordinate cofactors are distinguished from the four old g
cofactors and from the four new g cofactors. No quotient is divided in a finite
coefficient ring.

The accepted univariate-five output contains literal entries for the rr
seconds of the old four and new four g functions. This packet binds their
IDs and schema only; it does not extract or contract their values. It supplies
no r ell seconds. After that cache-presence inventory, sixteen individual
scalar labels remain unbound here. This is an inventory, not a proof that
sixteen new coefficients or source jets are necessary: exact source aliases,
coherent copies, shared response functionals or other accepted caches can
still reduce the request.

the restored h1 cofactor plus (Gamma28+Gamma55) times old g minus Gamma55 times
new g. Its contribution to the complete upper formula carries the negative
corrected a1 coefficient in the complementary plane variable. All native
