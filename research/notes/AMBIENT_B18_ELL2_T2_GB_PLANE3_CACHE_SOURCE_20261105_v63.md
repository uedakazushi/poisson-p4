# Existing-cache source formula for the old CB plane cubic

The current accepted NEGATIVE_BOUNDARY chain and the literal full-relative constructor give fCB28539-gB299650=sum_i zB_i*h_i. The seven zB functions are27897,27948,28203,28254,28437,28692,28794, and the seven coordinate quotient functions are327551,327554,327557,327560,327563,327566,327569, in that order. Their identities are bound to the actual pointed CB-F9 section, not inferred from names. The historical failed chain stem is not a premise.

All seven zB functions have zero point and plane r/ell first coefficients in the accepted actual FULL9 cache. All seven h functions have zero point. Thus the ordinary r²ell coefficient is exactly

    gB3 = fCB3 - sum_i(zB_i,rr*h_i,ell + zB_i,rell*h_i,r).

There is no zB3*h0 term and no h2 or h3 term. The h firsts are known; fCB28539 and all seven zB IDs are literal members of the accepted source164 raw33 readback catalog. The independently verified raw33-to-plane9 map selects raw basis24 for rr,25 for rell and30 for r²ell, with no contribution from another basis vector. Consequently the right side uses only existing exact cache values and known firsts, with no physical/gauge/higher implicit solve.

This packet verifies the source identity, orders and selectors only. It does not extract fCB3 or zB2 numbers, multiply them by h1, evaluate gB3, or evaluate an OLD/native/upper relation response. A later complete value bundle still needs its own height, ownership and independent source gate.
