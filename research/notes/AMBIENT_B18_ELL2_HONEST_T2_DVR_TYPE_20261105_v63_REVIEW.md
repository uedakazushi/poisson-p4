# Pure D-module type of T2: independent review

Use the same physical ell and D=k[[ell]]. The original freeR0 comparison
complex is D-flat because the formal coordinate change gives
R0=D[[eight variables]]. Its homology in degrees0,1 is C,K. The
low-degree Postnikov class lies in Ext_D²(C,K), which vanishes because
a DVR has global dimension one. Its low truncation therefore splits
noncanonically as C direct_sum K[1] in the derived category of D.

Derived tensor with A=D/(ell²) has Tor amplitude0,1. Homology in degree
at least two cannot contribute to degree one. Thus, as D-modules only,

    T2 is (K/ell²K) direct_sum C[ell²].

The accepted C2 type is A^6 direct_sum k². The free rank-four part of C
accounts for four of the A blocks in its quotient, so C[ell²] has type
A² direct_sum k². If j of the three K torsion exponents are at least
two, K/ell²K has type A^(4+j) direct_sum k^(3-j). Consequently T2 has
type A^(6+j) direct_sum k^(5-j), with0≤j≤3.

Its D-minimal generator number is always eleven, its ell-action rank is
6+j, and its length is17+j. In particular mu_B(T2)≤11. No value of j
is obtained. The split is not an R0-module, algebra, filtration or
natural-map split; natural reduction to Told is not identified with
the quotient by ell. No H0 exponent or nilpotence assertion follows.
