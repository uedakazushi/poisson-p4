# Existing-origin bridge for the ell² comparison

All768 origin receipts are replayed in their original32 kernel
coordinates, then read back in the full56 source coordinates. Exactly27
provided columns are independent; the other741 source combinations have
their own nonpivot coefficient one and only the27 selected pivot
columns otherwise. They form a full constant kernel basis. No new
point or elimination is used.

The new peered26-by56 matrix M(0) annihilates every original X(0) column.
Its selected26 minor is nonzero, so projection from kerM(0) to the30
complementary coordinates is an isomorphism. The same27 source columns
therefore remain source units in this free part. A numerical choice of
new target pivots is not needed for this conclusion.

The intrinsic native S0 is rebuilt from the original G8_C0 and complete
native Koszul/free signs. The point matrix L0=Res4(N^t) and its saved
inverse are checked in both orders, and L0*S0 agrees with every original
native origin column. For each of the741 combinations, its old part
is separately zero and S0 times its native part is separately zero.
Thus dropping the native L1 term in the forthcoming derivative gate is
a literal source identity for each input, not an assumption that the
full native derivative is zero. No X1 coefficient or mu(T2) is computed.
