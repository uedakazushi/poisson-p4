# Joint native third-source binding and conditional inventory

This packet binds the Told and Tnil native coefficient requests to the same
actual proper coordinates and canonical gauge. It computes no third source
or gauge coefficient and authorizes no new coefficient job. The counts below
are candidate upper inventories until the joint adjoints establish necessity.

The Told native map is Res4(N^t*S(G8)). The Tnil radical native map is
Res4(Srad35), with no N^t on that native block. In Tnil, scalar row20 means
generator h at C-row5 and trace mask0; it is not identified with Told's
physical C-row5. The checker verifies the common actual Cfree4 interface and
trace basis, and all nonzero entries of the radical h row: u0,...,u4 in
columns4,8,11,13,14, and -cr,-cl,-rl in columns30,31,32.

The actual source rules are shared: proper kappa9, its core entries

and the same tauA/tauB monics. The radical core coordinates are

by hashes. The external cut parameter rA is distinguished from r_core.

Told's current native triples are rA^3 and zi*rA^2 for i=0,1,4,5. Tnil's
native row20 requests the three triples with i=0,4,5, a strict subset. The
degree2 base20 cache, extended by all five triples, is a downward-closed
