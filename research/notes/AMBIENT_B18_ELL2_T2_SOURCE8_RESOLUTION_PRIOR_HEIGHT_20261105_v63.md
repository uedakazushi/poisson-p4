# Prior height proof for finite-resolution factor construction

This phase runs before any new67/182-span coefficient vector or signed quadratic response is built. It uses the already accepted frozen I2/I3 constant source vectors, fixed unit-basis receipt scalars, and literal raw differential coefficients. A positive pair(D,U) certifies that every rational denominator divides D and the sum of absolute D-cleared numerators is at most U. Addition uses LCM and positive sums; multiplication and division by known scalars propagate D/U exactly. There is no reduction of an envelope by a common content that could invalidate its denominator claim.

The source-representation bounds follow the frozen normalized-basis receipts without evaluating signed representations. The span bounds propagate coordinatewise positive envelopes through the same fixed triangular target elimination, then aggregate source representations by their L1 bound. The full free17/free42 projection correction is included before elimination. Only the separately proved exact zero free residuals may be omitted after processing pivot coordinates. All intermediate partial sums and source divisions are included. The Pi17*Kfirst linear input products are bounded separately.

The report explicitly says whether the prepared384-bit factor guard is covered. If it is not, the coefficient-construction job remains blocked until a refined source proof or explicit root decision; this phase does not raise a cap. No physical source, implicit coefficient, actual comparison value, signed G2/G3 factor vector, or quadratic response is evaluated.
