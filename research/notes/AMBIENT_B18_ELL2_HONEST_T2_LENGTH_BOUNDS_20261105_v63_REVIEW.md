# Ell² comparison length: independent pure DVR review

The original free comparison complex gives the exact derived reduction
sequence0→K/ell²K→T2→C[ell²]→0. The same physical parameter ell is used
throughout. For a finite module over D=k[[ell]], each cyclic torsion
summand D/(ell^a) contributes min(2,a) to both its ell²-kernel and its
ell²-quotient. The rank-four free summand contributes eight only to the
quotient. Thus the accepted length14 of C/ell²C gives lengthC[ell²]=6.

The accepted K has rank four and exactly three cyclic torsion summands.
Writing their positive exponents e1,e2,e3 gives

    lengthT2=14+sum min(2,e_i)=17+#{e_i>=2}.

Hence the only possible lengths are17,18,19,20. No exponent is evaluated.
Because the whole normalized E4 is ell-regular, the ell-torsion of D0
is exactly the torsion of K. K is finite over D, so this torsion has
finite k-length and is precisely H0_m(D0). Its length is sum e_i.
Consequently lengthT2=17, all three exponents equal one,
ell*H0_m(D0)=0, and lengthH0_m(D0)=3 are equivalent.

Annihilation by ell does not imply annihilation by the full maximal
ideal. No socle dimension, nil cube or exact nilpotence index follows.
This receipt verifies a formal corollary and evaluates no comparison
coefficient or actual T2 length.
