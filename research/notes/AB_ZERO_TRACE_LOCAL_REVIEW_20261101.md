## Completion-generation argument

The generalized lemma is valid in the Noetherian local algebraic setting used here. For the proper birational map from the normal incidence, D=H0(X,O_X) is finite over A and is its normalization. This is the applicable Noetherian [Stein factorization statement](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03H0).

Once the actual ideal m_A O_X is identified with I_C, the [formal-functions theorem](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02O7) identifies the completion of D with the inverse limit of H0(O_X/I_C^n). The all-degree H1 vanishings give surjective transition maps, with successive kernels H0(I_C^n/I_C^(n+1)). Degree-one generation and the surjection from m_A allow each successive error to be corrected by an element of m_A^n. The resulting series converges in A-completion. Hence A-completion surjects onto D-completion; finite generation and faithful completion force A=D. Beginning the approximation in degree n identifies the kernel filtration with m_A^n, so the associated-graded assertion also follows. This does not assume normality of A or of its completion in advance.
