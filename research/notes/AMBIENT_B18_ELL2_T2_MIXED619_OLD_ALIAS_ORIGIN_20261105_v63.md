# Twelve old arithmetic aliases have cached zero origins

Fifty of the62 literal C0 leaves in the mixed619 old callback profile have explicit cached origin bindings. The remaining twelve are addition nodes in the frozen source DAG. A selected-node streaming reader resolves their finite arithmetic closure to the same cached function boundaries and constants. It never decodes or retains the whole scalar DAG, invents a physical source coefficient, or redivides a regular quotient by a nonunit.

A scalar constant-arithmetic bound is proved before evaluating that selected origin closure. All62 origins are zero. This permits a later honest-second sensitivity computation to exploit positive-order products; their first cross-products remain and are not assigned zero. Neither alias first/second coefficients nor any actual619 relation is evaluated here.
