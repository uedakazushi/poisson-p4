# Withdrawal of the conditional syzygy application

The **arithmetic theorem** remains valid: the actual pair coefficient matrix
retaining columns 1..5 has rank2 at every a*P*J!=0 point. Its source replay,
QQ inverse identities, special-branch Bezout identities and archive are unchanged.

The companion note §2 assertion that this rank2 theorem plus ternary-pair
coprimality forces exactly two linear syzygies of the five quadrics is **withdrawn**.
The report/code field
`conditional_five_quadric_linear_syzygies_exactly2_if_ternary_pair_coprime`
is not an accepted theorem.

The omitted terms are genuine: vertex-linear components of the cofactors of the
pure-base scroll quadrics can contribute to the B*S and B*U coefficient equations;
for example, S*Q1 contains A*B*S/6075. Thus those equations cannot be read as the
retained 2x5 pair matrix applied only to K0,K3. An actual elimination in the scroll
coordinate module is still needed.

The §3 quadratic-syzygy reduction may be used only with the **separate additional
hypothesis** that the five-quadric linear-syzygy space is exactly the two known
scroll relations. This hypothesis has not been proved by the omitted-column
argument. No quad78 theorem follows from the current companion.

This erratum overrides the conditional prose/metadata while preserving the frozen
bytes for audit. The earlier whole pair rank2, constant five-quadric independence,
raw24 and original product41 results use different proofs and are unaffected.
Independent review identified this gap before the conditional claim was accepted.
