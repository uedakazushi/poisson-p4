V14 INDEPENDENT AUDIT: STABLE RANK-TWO EXHAUSTION
===============================================
All varieties and sheaves are over C. The statement audited is:

Let pi be a rank-two Poisson structure on P4 without a divisorial zero, and
let F be its saturated rank-two symplectic foliation. If H0(T_F)=0, then pi
has an ACTUAL Rat(1,1,3) or Rat(1,2,2) Jacobian presentation.

This result concerns the stable branch and does not by itself classify all
rank-two foliations with H0(T_F)!=0 or all rank-four components. It is stronger
than the historical low-degree-Casimir filters and makes the old stable
essential-five quartic / unbounded-minimal-degree frontier obsolete.

A. Canonical lift and five sections
----------------------------------
The canonical homogeneous quadratic divergence-free lift Pi is Poisson and
has affine rank2. Its rank2 follows from alpha=i_(Pi^2/2)Omega: alpha is closed,
has Euler weight5, and i_E alpha=0 because projective pi^2=0. Cartan gives
5alpha=d(i_E alpha)=0. Let G be saturated im(Pi:O(-1)^5→O(1)^5).
G is reflexive: the quotient of a locally free sheaf is torsion-free, and the
depth lemma makes the kernel S2. Since Pi is primitive, detG=O. Euler projection
identifies G with T_F outside codimension>=2, and thus globally by reflexivity.
Dualizing this embedding and using rank2 self-duality gives
 O^5→E=T_F(1), generating E in codimension1.
Let W be the image of these five sections. Its constant kernel is exactly
the space A1 of linear polynomial Casimirs. Thus dimW=5-dimA1.

B. A global section and a degree bound
--------------------------------------
A general section s in W has no divisorial zero and gives
 0→O→E→I_Z(2)→0.
The codimension-two scheme Z is unmixed. Locally at ambient codimension>=3,
reflexivity gives depthE>=2, hence depthI_Z>=2 and depthO_Z>=1, excluding
embedded associated primes. At codimension2 the sheaf is locally free.
Twisting by-1 and using H0(T_F)=0, H1(O(-1))=0 gives H0(I_Z(1))=0.
Determinants s wedge s_i are quadrics vanishing scheme-theoretically on Z;
they have no common divisor, because the five sections generate in codimension1
and s has no divisorial zero. Choose two coprime such quadrics q1,q2.
Then Z⊂Y=V(q1,q2), a (2,2) complete intersection. Thus degZ<=4.
If degZ=0, unmixedness gives Z empty and the extension splits O⊕O(2), violating
stability. If degZ=1, unmixedness makes Z a plane, violating H0(I_Z(1))=0.
Therefore degZ=2,3,4; equivalently c2(T_F)=1,2,3. No Bogomolov equality theorem
or stability of the restriction to a linear plane is needed.

C. Degree4 and degree3 branches
-------------------------------
If degZ=4, Z=Y: same generic lengths inside the unmixed complete intersection
force equality (the difference would be a smaller-dimensional submodule of
O_Y). Resolving I_Z and the extension gives
 0→O(-2)→O^3→E→0,
so h0(E)=3. Hence dimA1>=2, and the previously proved exact low-center lemma
gives actual Rat113.

If degZ=3, the residual colon in Y has degree1 and is an unmixed plane H.
This linkage does not require prior local Cohen–Macaulayness of Z. In the
Gorenstein complete-intersection local ring A, the quotients by annihilator
ideals embed in finite direct sums of A and are unmixed. At minimal primes,
Artinian-Gorenstein double-annihilator duality holds. Since unmixed ideals are
determined by those localizations, Ann Ann(I_Z/I_Y)=I_Z/I_Y globally.
Writing H=(L1,L2) and qi=Ai L1+Bi L2 gives the reverse colon explicitly:
 I_Z=(q1,q2,A1 B2-A2 B1).
This is the Hilbert–Burch ideal, with resolution
 0→O(-3)^2→O(-2)^3→I_Z→0.
Lifting its twist by2 into the extension yields
 0→O(-1)^2→O^4→E→0.
Thus h0(E)=4 and dimA1>=1. If dimA1>=2, use Rat113 as before. Otherwise choose
L=t as the unique linear center. The four actual sections span all H0(E), so
dualizing the Steiner presentation identifies T_F inside O(1)^4 as the kernel
of a two-by-four matrix of homogeneous linear forms. Consequently
 beta=i_Pi Omega = constant * dt wedge theta1 wedge theta2,
where theta_i are vertical one-forms on four variables with coefficients linear
in those variables and t. No scalar polynomial factor is present because both
the primitive bivector and the minimal minors have determinant degree zero.

Closed-form lemma (independently audited): Put A=d_y theta1, B=d_y theta2.
These are constant two-forms and closedness says A wedge theta2=theta1 wedge B.
If their pencil contains a rank4 form, change basis to make A nondegenerate.
Define the homogeneous linear vertical field X by i_X A=theta1. Contracting
the identity gives
 theta2(X) A = -theta1 wedge (i_X B+theta2).
The right side has rank at most2; A has rank4, so theta2(X)=0. Also theta1(X)=0.
Thus X is a nonzero global tangent linear field, contradicting H0(T_F)=0.
If A,B are independent but every member has rank<=2, their decomposable forms
share a factor; choose A=dx wedge dy, B=dx wedge dz. The same identity forces
both theta_i to have no dw component, giving tangent constant field partial_w
(multiply by t for a global linear field). If B is proportional to A!=0,
replace theta2 to make B=0 and take A=dx wedge dy. Then theta2 involves only
dx,dy, and X=(theta1)_w partial_z-(theta1)_z partial_w is tangent. If X=0,
partial_z is tangent instead. Both contradict stability. Therefore A=B=0.
Integrating their linear coefficients gives homogeneous quadrics Q1,Q2 with
theta_i=d_y Qi. Thus this branch has actual Rat122 presentation.

D. Degree2: why the zero surface is reduced
------------------------------------------
The rank-zero locus of W⊗O→E cannot contain a surface S. Near its generic point
E is locally free and all five sections vanish, so every quadratic Plücker
coefficient of Pi vanishes to second order along S. Each linear derivative of
each coefficient then vanishes on S. The coefficients lie in the symmetric
square of the linear ideal of span(S). Since dimS=2, primitiveness forces spanS
to be a plane and Pi to depend on only two linear variables u,v.
Its ten primitive binary quadrics have no common point of P1, so they define
a map P1→Gr(2,5) of Plücker degree2. The pulled-back tautological bundle splits
O(-a)⊕O(-b), a,b>=0, a+b=2. Pulling back to P4 outside (u=v=0), twisting by1,
and extending reflexively makes T_F split O(1-a)⊕O(1-b), contradicting stability.
Thus rank-zero locus has dimension<=1.

A general section has no surface zero component inside the rank-one locus:
that locus has finitely many surface components, and vanishing identically
on one imposes a proper linear condition on W. Every surface component of Z
therefore meets the rank-two evaluation open set. There E is a vector bundle
generated by W, so generic-section Bertini makes Z generically smooth and
reduced. Unmixedness then makes Z reduced everywhere.
A reduced pure surface of degree2 not contained in a hyperplane is exactly
two planes meeting at one point. An irreducible degree2 surface spans P3;
two planes meeting in a line also span P3. Thus, after coordinates,
 Z=V(a,b) union V(c,d) in P4_(a,b,c,d,t).

E. All reflexive extensions are null-correlation models
------------------------------------------------------
For this Z, Mayer–Vietoris and duality give
 sheaf Ext1(I_Z(2),O)=O_(V(a,b)) directsum O_(V(c,d)).
The higher cohomology of Hom(I_Z(2),O)=O(-2) vanishes in the relevant degrees,
so global Ext1(I_Z(2),O)=C^2. A reflexive extension must have both components
nonzero, since on each plane's generic codimension2 point the middle sheaf
must be locally free. Block-diagonal coordinate scalings change the two local
normal determinants independently, so the open set of such extension pairs
is one orbit up to coordinate changes and extension rescaling.

One representative is the null-correlation monad:
 0→O(-1) --v--> O^4 --v^t J--> O(1)→0,
where v=(a,b,c,d)^t and J12=J34=1 is symplectic. Let Ntilde denote its middle
cohomology, and E=Ntilde(1). It is reflexive (the only possible non-bundle point
is a=b=c=d=0 and the local Koszul depths are at least2). The section represented
by (a,b,0,0) has precisely zero scheme Z; the determinant quadrics generate
(ac,ad,bc,bd). It has nonzero extension class on both planes and so represents
all reflexive extensions up to the indicated changes. The linear syzygies of
v^tJ have dimension6; quotienting by the radial section leaves h0(E)=5.

F. Complete-section Plücker normal form and changes of basis
-----------------------------------------------------------
Use primitive alternating forms on C4 with basis
 F0=e12-e34, F1=e13, F2=e14, F3=e23, F4=e24;
put K_i=J^-1 F_i. Their Clifford relations are
 K_i K_j+K_j K_i=2 q_ij I,
where q00=1, q14=q41=1/2, q23=q32=-1/2 and all other entries vanish.
The five complete sections of E evaluate as K_w v modulo C v in v^perp/C v.
The matrix B(v) of w→K_wv is
 [ a, 0,0,-c,-d;
   b, c,d, 0, 0;
  -c, 0,a, 0, b;
  -d,-a,0,-b, 0 ].
It has rank3 for every v!=0: its3x3 minors span all20 cubic monomials.
Clifford anticommutation shows that kerB(v) is the q-orthogonal complement of
the kernel of the five-section evaluation map. Thus q identifies it with
the dual-image plane of E. Its Plücker bivector has pair-order
 01,02,03,04,12,13,14,23,24,34
and entries
 P0(v)=(bd,-bc,-ad,ac,b^2,d^2,-ab-cd,ab-cd,c^2,a^2).
All Pfaffians vanish and P0^t q P0=0. These ten quadrics span Sym^2 C4^*, so
P3→OGr(2,5) is the2-Veronese map in Plücker coordinates and an isomorphism
onto OGr(2,5) (both are irreducible dimension3). Any orthogonal change of q
therefore induces a projective linear change of v, since Aut(P3)=PGL4.

The original five sections need not span all h0(E). In complete-section bases,
the actual canonical bivector has form C P0(Ax) C^t, with rankA=4 and C a
constant5x5 matrix of rank dimW. RankC<=3 gives dimA1>=2 and Rat113. If rankC5,
an ambient coordinate change absorbs C. If rankC4, its kernel line in the
orthogonal five-space is isotropic or nonisotropic. Orthogonal transformations
(absorbed by a linear change of spinor variables) and ambient coordinate
changes normalize C to diag(0,1,1,1,1) in the nonisotropic case or
diag(1,0,1,1,1) in the isotropic case. No other rank4 case exists.

G. Exact divergence audit and the two universal quadratic centers
----------------------------------------------------------------
All results below were independently computed with exact rational arithmetic.
For v=A x with A4x5 and rankA4, write
 div_x P0(Ax) = D(A) v.
D is a linear map from the20 matrix entries of A to20 coefficients. Its matrix
has determinant16, so the rank5 complete-section case cannot be divergence-free.

For the nonisotropic rank4 projection, the corresponding linear system has
rank16. Its kernel has only column0 nonzero, giving rankA<=1; impossible.

For the isotropic rank4 projection (drop row/column1), the system has rank12.
Its full eight-parameter kernel is
 A=[ 0, r0,  0, 0,-r4;
    -r6,r1,-r4, 0, r2;
     0, r3,  0, 0, r6;
     r4,r5,  0,r6, r7 ].
Put t=x1. Two homogeneous quadratic Casimirs are
 Q1=r0*t*x0-r4*x0*x4-r3*t*x3-r6*x3*x4-r5*t*x4-r7*x4^2/2,
 Q2=-r3*t*x0-r6*x0*x4+r0*t*x2-r4*x2*x4+r1*t*x4+r2*x4^2/2.
Let a,b,c,d be the four rows of A x. Then
 d_yQ1=a dx0-c dx3-d dx4,
 d_yQ2=-c dx0+a dx2+b dx4.
The complementary3x3 Jacobian minors of (t,Q1,Q2) are exactly -Pdrop1(Ax).
The code verifies every entry and all centrality equations polynomially in
all eight parameters, without any nonvanishing assumption. For rankA4 the
bivector is nonzero, hence this is an actual Rat122 presentation.

H. Consequences for the old frontier
-----------------------------------
The three possible degrees of Z are exhausted. Every stable, no-divisorial-zero
rank2 Poisson structure has an actual Rat113 or Rat122 representation.
In particular A1!=0; the graded center-field gcd m is1, not5. The hypothetical
stable branches A1=A2=A3=0, or only powers of one linear center through degree3,
produce no additional Poisson structures. The historical quartic/higher-degree
center searches are superseded for the purpose of classifying this branch.
Polynomiality of the ENTIRE center ring at every special rational member is
not asserted by this proof, but no longer obstructs this Poisson classification.
The six full generic-rank2 components and the total lower bound57 are unchanged
by this stable exhaustion alone; the nonstable rank2 alternatives and full
rank4 S61 must still be audited separately.
