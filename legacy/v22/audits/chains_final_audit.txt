1. Audit of line_nodes.tex
-------------------------
The zero/nonzero slope dichotomy is covered: one zero slope forces the
ambient linearization nilpotent, hence the other tangent slope is also
zero. The proof therefore correctly reduces to a,b nonzero.

The six possible line spectra are
  a {1,-h/2,-m/3,h/2+m/3-1}, h in {1,2}, m in {0,1,2}.
The common-spectrum comparison gives only equal tangent eigenvalues or
b=-a, h=h'=2, m=m'. Equal nonzero slopes are impossible because the tangent
eigenvalue a has multiplicity one in each of the six spectra. Independent
tangent directions would give two eigenvectors.

With both conormal brackets zero at the point, the linear Poisson tensor
is ell(z,w) d/dx wedge d/dy. This follows from the two abelian conormal
hyperplanes, the vanishing of pi on both axes, and the fact that the
linear tensor must vanish on those axes. It remains valid when additional
support branches pass through the point.

m=0: The simple zero of the global rank-one line pencil supplies a nonzero
xy coefficient in pi_2^{zw}. Multiplication by ell produces an order-one
mixed Hessian term along a line. No other linear Poisson component can
cancel it. This contradicts h=2. If ell=0, the Pfaffian starts in degree
four, contradicting the nonvanishing cubic scalar at the point.

m=1: For weights (3,-3,-1,1), the six displayed quadratic components form
the complete weight-zero template. The coefficients BJ and
B(C+G+K)-2DI are indeed the xyz and wz^2 coefficients of the xyz Jacobi
component, with the displayed sign conventions. J is nonzero from the
line pencil. Both cubic flags force D and I nonzero. Hence the two Jacobi
coefficients contradict one another. No exceptional coefficient cell was
discarded.

m=2: The integrated proof uses ONLY the corrected projective argument.
The obsolete assertion that the xyzw coefficient of F_4 must vanish is
absent. For weights (3,-3,-2,2), no quadratic monomial has one of the four
coordinate weights. Thus Z_2 has no weight-zero part. The correctly signed
identity pi_3=(1/5)E wedge Z_2 implies the same for pi_3. Since pi_2 has
weight zero and the total volume weight is zero, the mixed Pfaffian F_5

valid contradiction using only linear changes of coordinates; no formal
