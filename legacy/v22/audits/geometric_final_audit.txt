Applied insertion 1: SS with B_T(p)=0 and B_L(p) nonzero
-------------------------------------------------------
Location: twisted_cubic.tex, immediately before the two possibilities
(rho,k,m_L)=(-3/2,2,2),(-2,3,0).

Comparing just the line h=1 spectrum with the TC tangent eigenvalue and the
leading centre weight leaves FOUR formal possibilities:
  (-2,3,0), (-2,3,1), (-3/2,2,2), (-2,3,2).
Thus the present phrase "leading-center weight ... spectrum comparison" is
too compressed to justify the two-case list on its own.

The needed relation is already proved by the isotropy argument in line_conic:
let the TC tangent weight be 1, the line tangent weight rho, H_T abelian and
H_L Heisenberg, with common two-dimensional abelian ideal N. The nonzero
nilpotent map ad(dt)|_N has centre K of weight nu=-rho/2. Since dt has weight
1 modulo N, its nonzero map N/K -> K shifts weight by 1. Hence N/K has weight
nu-1, and the ambient spectrum is
  {1,rho,-rho/2,-rho/2-1}.
Also ker(ad(dt):H_T->H_T) has only weights rho and -rho/2. The leading TC
centre lies in that kernel and has weight -(k+1)/2. Compare these relations
with the line h=1 spectrum, with 1<=k<=3 and 0<=m_L<=2. Exactly the two stated
possibilities remain. No semisimplicity or identification of the centre with
the actual external hyperplane conormal is used.

Exact rational finite comparison saved as final_ss_reduction_audit.json.
The comparison records the extra weak candidates and the two correct strong
candidates, so it specifically tests the missing justification.

Applied clarification 2: two-line Hessian coordinates
------------------------------------------------------
Location: line_nodes.tex, before the m=0/1/2 split.

After b=-a and h=h'=2, the limiting conormal centres have weights -a and a.
Those two ambient eigenvalues are simple, including the m=0 case. Choose
linear coordinates preserving the two axes so that the constant Hessian
centre forms are dy on the x-axis and dx on the y-axis. The normal 2x2 block

directions is valid in these chosen coordinates, not arbitrary axis
coordinates. This linear coordinate choice preserves the projective cubic
identity and the straight line parametrizations.
