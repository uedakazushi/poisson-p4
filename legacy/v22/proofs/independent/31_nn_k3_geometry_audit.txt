Independent audit of the 31 NN k=3 initial reduction
===================================================

Audited against the raw matrices in root/e8_jets/31_external.json.
The normal-coordinate ordering for the twisted cubic is (x2,x3,x4).
This audit concerns the geometric initial slice, not the later exact linear
certificate (which is audited separately).

Input: the two modular residues are normalized to t^2, both conormal brackets
vanish at the node, and the twisted-cubic bracket vanishes there to order 3.
Use the already-established node substitutions
z36=z38=z30=z31=z25=z32=z22=z33=0,
z37=z39=1, z14=-z35.

1. Coefficients below order 3 of the twisted-cubic bracket give:
 B01[t^2]=3 z17                 => z17=0;
 B11[t]=-2 z17+z27              => z27=0;
 B11[t^2]=-3 z18                => z18=0;
 B02[t^2]=z34                   => z34=0;
 B12[t]=-z26                    => z26=0;
 B12[t^2]=-3 z28-2 z35          => 3 z28+2 z35=0;
 B22[t]=z21, B22[t^2]=3 z23      => z21=z23=0.

2. The leading symmetric rank-one matrix B/t^3 has entry00=0 and
 entry02=3 z28+4 z35. Its 2x2 minor on indices(0,2) is minus the
 square of entry02. At a complex closed point, this forces
 3 z28+4 z35=0. Combined with step1: z28=z35=0 and hence z14=0.

3. The internal 2x2 block becomes
 t^3 z9 [[4t^2,-2t],[-2t,1]].
 If z9=0, rank one forces both internal rows/columns to be zero, so
 the conormal center is purely the external hyperplane covector along
 the entire twisted cubic. Then the Hessian of F|H vanishes along T,
 F|H has multiplicity at least3 along T, and every secant line lies in
 V(F|H) because 2*3>5. Since secants fill H, F|H=0, contradicting
 normality of the actual quintic. Therefore z9!=0.

4. Rank one with the displayed nonzero internal block implies
 row0+2t row1=0. Its final-column entry is exactly
 B02+2t B12 = t^4(z19-2 z29)+t^5 z20.
 Hence z20=0 and z19=2 z29.

5. The line bracket has entry00=t z21=0, entry01=t z23=0,
 and entry02=t z24. Rank one gives z24=0.

These are precisely the initial reductions recorded in
chains/31_C_NN_k3_certificate.json. No parameter division occurs except
using the geometrically proved nonzero z9 to identify the row direction;
equivalently the row relation follows after localization at this required
nonzero scalar. No branch with z9=0 is discarded without the separate
hyperplane/secant exclusion in step3.

Audit result: PASS. No assumption that the actual hyperplane covector is
an eigenvector of the modular linearization is used.
